## 2026-07-02 — OOM investigation: browser leak fix deployed, BS4 cycle leak found (fix pending)
Diagnosed two distinct OOM causes from `app.log` and live container inspection: (1) `RargbCrawler`'s Chromium process was never closed per `crawl_rargb` trigger — fixed with `RargbCrawler.close()` / `BrowserDriver.close()` / `try/finally` in `service.py`, rebuilt and redeployed, confirmed zero leaked processes after 3 test triggers. Also surfaced a separate, unrelated backlog gap (movies inserted with no `predict`/`crawl_imdb` Kafka message produced, likely from a transient Kafka outage) — backfilled manually via `/produce/predict` and `/produce/imdb`; a periodic self-healing backfill thread was proposed but not built.
